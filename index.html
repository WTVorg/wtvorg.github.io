Shorts Bot

A private desktop application that publishes short videos to its owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

Shorts Bot runs on its owner's computer. It produces short vertical videos in two ways: original, source-checked educational Shorts (AI-written script, every factual claim checked against web sources, text-to-speech narration, licensed stock footage) and clips cut from long recordings the owner has explicit permission to edit. A person reviews the output before anything is published.

How it uses YouTube

The application uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channels (private by default), to confirm processing status, to read the owner's own public statistics and retention metrics, and to see which public topics are currently popular. It has no other users, collects no data about anyone other than its owner, and does not automate views, likes or comments.

By using this application the owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
