Shorts Bot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

Shorts Bot is a private desktop application that runs on its owner's computer and publishes short videos to YouTube channels owned by the same person. It has no other users.

What the application accesses

Shorts Bot uses YouTube API Services with the owner's permission, through Google sign-in, for:

  • Uploading videos the application produced to the owner's channels (YouTube upload scope).
  • Reading the owner's channels to confirm which channel is signed in, to check whether an upload finished processing, and to read the public view and like counts of the owner's own uploads (YouTube read-only scope).
  • Reading the owner's own analytics (average view duration, view percentage, subscribers gained) for the owner's own videos (YouTube Analytics read-only scope).
  • Public YouTube searches to see which topics are popular; only the titles of a few videos are kept as a note on why a topic was chosen.

By using the application, the owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored locally on the owner's computer: the sign-in token for the owner's own Google account, the IDs, titles and statistics of the owner's own uploads, the scripts, sources and video files the application produced, and log files. Nothing is sent to any third party other than the API providers the application calls (Google/YouTube, the AI model provider used for script writing, and the stock-footage provider). No data about viewers or any other person is collected.

Deletion and revocation

  • Stored YouTube data (upload IDs and statistics) is refreshed or deleted within 30 days of no longer being needed; the owner can delete all of it at any time by deleting the application's data folder.
  • The owner can revoke the application's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token then stops working and is removed on the next run.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact address shown on the application's Google sign-in screen.
